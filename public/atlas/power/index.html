Atlas

Phrasebook Time Power Driving

Most modern chargers handle any voltage. Check the label: “100–240V” means you only need a plug adapter, never a converter. Old hair dryers and kettles are the exception.

Continental Europe

Type C / E / F · 220V · 50Hz

France, Spain, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Portugal, Greece, Czechia, Hungary — most of mainland Europe.

UK & former colonies

Type G · 220V · 50Hz

United Kingdom, Ireland, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, Malta, Cyprus. The big rectangular three-pin.

North America & Japan

Type A / B · 110V · 60Hz

United States, Canada, Mexico, Japan, parts of Latin America.

Australia, NZ, China

Type I · 220V · 50Hz

Australia, New Zealand, China, Argentina. Angled flat pins forming an upside-down V.

Italy

Type L (some F) · 220V · 50Hz

Italian outlets vary. Type C Europlugs usually fit, but some hotels still have only Type L. Worth grabbing the Italian-specific adapter.

Switzerland

Type J · 220V · 50Hz

Swiss-only Type J. Slim Europlugs sometimes fit, sturdier ones don’t. Bring the Swiss adapter.
