Power

Most modern chargers handle any voltage — check the label for “100–240V” and you only need a plug adapter, never a converter.

Continental EuropeType C / E / F · 220V

France, Spain, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Portugal, Greece, Czechia, Hungary, the Nordics — most of mainland Europe. Italy mostly works with Europlugs but bring an extra adapter just in case.

UK & former coloniesType G · 220V

United Kingdom, Ireland, Hong Kong, Singapore, Malaysia, Malta, Cyprus. The big rectangular three-pin.

North America & JapanType A / B · 110V

United States, Canada, Mexico, Japan, parts of Latin America. Flat parallel pins.

Australia, NZ, ChinaType I · 220V

Australia, New Zealand, China, Argentina. Angled flat pins forming an upside-down V.

Southeast AsiaMixed · 220V

The honest answer: it’s mixed. Indonesia and Vietnam mostly use Type C/F (Europlug). Thailand mixes Type A, B, and C. The Philippines uses A/B/C. A universal travel adapter saves the headache.

SwitzerlandType J · 220V

Swiss-only Type J. Slim Europlugs sometimes fit, sturdier ones don’t. Bring the Swiss adapter.

Driving

If you’re going to a former British colony or Japan, expect to drive on the left. Otherwise, right.

Drive on the left~75 countries

UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Hong Kong, India, South Africa, Kenya, Jamaica, Cyprus, Malta — plus most former British colonies.

Drive on the righteverywhere else

Continental Europe, North America, most of Latin America, China, Middle East, most of Africa.
